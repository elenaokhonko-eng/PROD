# FAQ & contact page copy

## Intro
Honest answers to the questions people ask us most — usually at 2am,
usually worried. If yours isn't here, contact us below or on WhatsApp.

## Getting started

**Who can use GuideBuoy?**
Anyone in Singapore dealing with a scam, fraud, an unauthorised
transaction, or a dispute with a bank, telco, or platform. Caregivers and
nominees can help someone else with their case, with permission.

**Do I need an account before telling my story?**
No. Type or record first — your story stays on your device. You only
create an account when you want to save it as a case and add evidence.

**Is Singpass required?**
No. You can sign up with Singpass or with email — whichever is easier.

**What if my English isn't strong, or I find forms hard?**
Speak your story instead of typing, in your own words. Lumi asks one
question at a time, "I'm not sure" is always allowed, and nothing
auto-advances while you're still reading.

## Your report

**Which organisations can I use the report with?**
Your bank or financial institution, the Police (SPF e-services),
ScamShield, and FIDReC. The same organised facts, reformatted for each
destination — you never retell the story.

**What is the evidence checklist?**
A live list of the proof that strengthens your case — what you already
have, what's recommended, and what's missing — with links to upload each
item right next to it.

**Can I edit what Lumi writes?**
Yes, always. Everything Lumi extracts or summarises is labelled, and
every field is editable. You have the final word on your own story.

**Is the pathway signal a decision?**
No. It's a preliminary, informational signal about which route your case
is likely to take. Only the bank, FIDReC, or the authorities make actual
decisions.

## Packs & pricing

**Is it really free?**
The User Pack — story, evidence organisation, focused questions, and your
free draft — costs nothing, no card required. Paid packs exist only if
you choose them.

**What's the difference between the Bank Pack and the FIDReC Pack?**
The Bank Pack is the complete self-serve complaint report for your
financial institution. The FIDReC Pack (SGD 800, one-off) adds
human-reviewed preparation of your submission to FIDReC, after the bank's
final response or the four-week wait. An optional 30-minute specialist
consultation is SGD 99.

**Will I be charged anything recurring?**
Never. Every paid item is a one-off payment, shown in full before you
pay, via Stripe.

**Do you guarantee I'll win?**
No one honest can. We make your case clear, complete, and well-organised.
Decisions belong to the banks, FIDReC, and the authorities.

## Privacy & trust

**How is my data protected?**
Encrypted storage, consent-logged actions, PDPA-aligned practices, and
secure payments via Stripe (we never see your card details).

**Who can see my report?**
Only you — plus anyone you explicitly invite as a collaborator, and the
specialist you explicitly choose to share with. Every access is logged.

**Can I delete everything?**
Yes — one click, "Delete my report", and it's gone. No retention games.
