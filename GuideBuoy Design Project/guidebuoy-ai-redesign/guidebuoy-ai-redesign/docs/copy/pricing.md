# Pricing page copy

## Intro
Free to start. Paid only when you choose more. Every price is a one-off
payment — no subscriptions, no hidden recurring charges, ever. No pack
guarantees an outcome; each one makes your case clearer, stronger, and
easier for the right people to act on.

## The three packs

### User Pack — Free
For anyone who needs to get their story in order, right now.
- Tell your story by typing or voice
- Guided evidence upload (bank statements, screenshots, SMS, receipts)
- Lumi's focused questions to fill the gaps
- Free draft: story summary + evidence checklist + preliminary pathway signal
- Edit and reuse your draft anytime
- Delete everything anytime

Does not include: the full complaint report, or any human review.

**Start free — no card required.**

### FI Pack — SGD 18 one-off payment
*(Price shown at checkout; no surprises.)*
For disputes with a bank or financial institution that need a complete,
professional complaint.
- Everything in the User Pack
- Full complaint report: executive summary, chronology, disputed
  transactions with totals, responsibility points, requested resolution
- Evidence map linking every fact to its document
- Structured for your bank's complaint process — and reusable later for
  FIDReC if needed
- Copy, print, download, and update as your case develops

Does not include: human review, specialist time, or FIDReC case-pack
preparation.

**Upgrade from your free draft when you're ready.**

### FIDReC Pack — SGD 800 one-off
For cases heading to the Financial Industry Disputes Resolution Centre
after the bank's final response or the four-week wait.
- Everything in the FI Pack
- Human-reviewed case-pack preparation: evidence organisation, chronology,
  and issue framing for FIDReC's format
- Guidance through each step of the submission
- Add a **30-minute Scam & Fraud Specialist consultation — SGD 99** —
  one-to-one, before or during preparation
- Not sure you qualify? Request a free handoff review — a human follows
  up within 1–2 business days

FIDReC filing itself is free. You're paying for preparation, not the
filing.

## Honest fine print (styled as a calm callout, not legal grey text)
- We are not a law firm and this is not legal advice.
- No pack guarantees that a bank, FIDReC, or any authority will rule in
  your favour.
- Refunds: if a paid report cannot be generated, you are not charged again
  to retry; if we can't deliver, you get your money back.
- Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see your card
