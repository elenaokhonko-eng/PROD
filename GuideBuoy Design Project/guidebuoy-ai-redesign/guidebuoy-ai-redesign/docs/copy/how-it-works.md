# How it works — page copy

## Intro
From "something went wrong" to "here's my organised case" — in six calm
steps. You never have to explain yourself twice.

## The six steps

### 1. Tell Lumi what happened
Type or speak your story in plain language. There's no wrong way to say
it. Lumi listens, transcribes, and gently identifies what kind of case
this is. If your situation isn't something we can help with yet, we'll
tell you honestly and point you to the right place.

### 2. Save your case and add your proof
Create a free account (Singpass or email). Your story transfers over
safely — nothing is lost. Then add what you have: screenshots, bank
statements, SMS messages, receipts, reference numbers. Even one document
is enough to begin.

### 3. Answer a few focused questions
Lumi reads your story and documents, then asks only what's missing — one
question at a time. "I'm not sure" is always an acceptable answer. Your
documents keep being processed in the background while you answer.

### 4. Receive your free draft (User Pack)
You get three things, free: a clear summary of your story, an evidence
checklist showing what you have and what's still missing, and a
preliminary signal about which path your case is likely to take. You can
edit everything. All of it lives in your **Report Hub** — one calm
workspace where your story, evidence, next steps, and drafts stay
together and synced, so you never feel lost between visits.

### 5. Choose your next step
If your case involves a bank or financial institution, you can generate
the full **Bank Pack** report — a complete, structured complaint with
chronology, disputed transactions, and what you're asking for. Or keep
using your free draft on your own. Both are fine.

### 6. Get human help if you want it
If the bank's response doesn't resolve things, the **FIDReC Pack** helps
you prepare a submission to the Financial Industry Disputes Resolution
Centre — with optional one-to-one specialist support. Filing with FIDReC
itself is free.

## The paths GuideBuoy understands (plain words)
- **Bank or financial institution** — most cases start here; the bank gets
  the first chance to respond.
- **FIDReC** — free, independent dispute resolution if you're still
  dissatisfied after the bank's final response (or after four weeks).
- **IMDA / telco** — for scam calls, SMS, and telco-assisted fraud.
- **Police & ScamShield** — for reporting the crime itself and checking
  suspicious contacts.
- **Crypto / overseas platforms** — harder cases; we're honest about
  what's realistic and show the options that still exist.

## Waiting is not wasted time
If you need to wait out the bank's response window, GuideBuoy gives you a
four-week tracker: log every call and reference number, prepare your
evidence, and get a gentle reminder the day you become eligible to
escalate.
