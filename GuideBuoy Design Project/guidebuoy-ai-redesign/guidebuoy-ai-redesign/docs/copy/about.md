## The story
GuideBuoy AI exists because of a simple, unfair reality: after a scam,
the person who has just lost money is the person expected to do the
hardest work — retelling a painful story, in formal language, to a bank,
then the Police, then maybe FIDReC, each with their own form, their own
deadlines, their own jargon.

We think that burden should sit on the system, not on the victim. So we
built Singapore's complaint helper: tell your story once, and let Lumi —
our lighthouse AI — organise it into one report that every agency can
use. A buoy doesn't pull you out of the water. It holds its position,
keeps its light on, and shows you the way in. That's the product.

## What we are (and what we're not)
- A Singapore company — GuideBuoy AI SG Pte Ltd, UEN 202545875C — building
  a public-good utility with optional paid services. Our MVP is live at
  guidebuoyai.sg, and all payments are processed securely by Stripe.
- PDPA-aligned and privacy-first: encrypted storage, consent-logged
  actions, and a one-click "delete my report" that actually deletes.
- Responsible AI: Lumi organises and clarifies; it never decides your
  case, never gives legal advice, and always shows you what it extracted
  so you can correct it.
- Not a law firm, not a government agency, not affiliated with FIDReC,
  MAS, IMDA, or the Police.
