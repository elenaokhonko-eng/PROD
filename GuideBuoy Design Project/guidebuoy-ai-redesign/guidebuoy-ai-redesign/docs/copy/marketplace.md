# Marketplace page copy

## Intro
Human help, only when you want it. The helper stays free — these people
step in only when you ask. Free support always comes first; anything paid
is clearly marked, and charges appear only when you accept.

## Free help first

### Pro-bono legal clinics (via SAL-linked clinics)
For vulnerable users or anyone who needs human legal guidance beyond the
helper. We make a warm handover — you don't have to explain your case
from scratch.

### Social service partners
Social workers who can help with reporting, recovery steps, and
safeguarding — especially if the scam has affected your daily life or
someone you care for.
[Connect me to a social worker →]

### Wellbeing support
Trauma-informed counsellors for the stress, embarrassment, and anxiety
that often follow a scam. These feelings are normal, and help is free.
[Get wellbeing support →]

## Paid specialists (clearly marked, optional)

### Lawyer case review
A short, fixed-scope review of your documents and likely next steps
before you escalate. Useful when the amounts are large or the facts are
tangled.
[Book a review →]

### Dispute coach / case preparation
Specialists who help structure your evidence, close gaps, and frame the
issues — the same craft that goes into our FIDReC Pack.
[Work with a coach →]

### Cybersecurity specialist report
Forensic review of links, malware, and transaction traces — strengthens
your report when the scam was technical.
[Request a cyber report →]

support around those packs — never a requirement.
