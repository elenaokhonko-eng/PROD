rocket_launch Getting started

Who can use GuideBuoy? expand_more

Anyone in Singapore dealing with a scam, fraud, an unauthorised transaction, or a dispute with a bank, telco, or platform. Caregivers and nominees can help someone else with their case, with permission.

Do I need an account before telling my story? expand_more

No. Type or record first — your story stays on your device. You only create an account when you want to save it as a case and add evidence.

Is Singpass required? expand_more

No. You can sign up with Singpass or with email — whichever is easier.

What if my English isn't strong, or I find forms hard? expand_more

Speak your story instead of typing, in your own words. Lumi asks one question at a time, "I'm not sure" is always allowed, and nothing auto-advances while you're still reading.

description Your report

Which organisations can I use the report with? expand_more

Your bank or financial institution, the Police (SPF e-services), ScamShield, and FIDReC. The same organised facts, reformatted for each destination — you never retell the story.

What is the evidence checklist? expand_more

A live list of the proof that strengthens your case — what you already have, what's recommended, and what's missing — with links to upload each item right next to it.

Can I edit what Lumi writes? expand_more

Yes, always. Everything Lumi extracts or summarises is labelled, and every field is editable. You have the final word on your own story.

Is the pathway signal a decision? expand_more

No. It's a preliminary, informational signal about which route your case is likely to take. Only the bank, FIDReC, or the authorities make actual decisions.

sell Packs & pricing

Is it really free? expand_more

The User Pack — story, evidence organisation, focused questions, and your free draft — costs nothing, no card required. Paid packs exist only if you choose them.

What's the difference between the Bank Pack and the FIDReC Pack? expand_more

The Bank Pack is the complete self-serve complaint report for your financial institution. The FIDReC Pack (SGD 800, one-off) adds human-reviewed preparation of your submission to FIDReC, after the bank's final response or the four-week wait. An optional 30-minute specialist consultation is SGD 99.

Will I be charged anything recurring? expand_more

Never. Every paid item is a one-off payment, shown in full before you pay, via Stripe.

Do you guarantee I'll win? expand_more

No one honest can. We make your case clear, complete, and well-organised. Decisions belong to the banks, FIDReC, and the authorities.

shield Privacy & trust

How is my data protected? expand_more

Encrypted storage, consent-logged actions, PDPA-aligned practices, and secure payments via Stripe (we never see your card details).

Who can see my report? expand_more

Only you — plus anyone you explicitly invite as a collaborator, and the specialist you explicitly choose to share with. Every access is logged.

Can I delete everything? expand_more

Yes — one click, "Delete my report", and it's gone. No retention games.
