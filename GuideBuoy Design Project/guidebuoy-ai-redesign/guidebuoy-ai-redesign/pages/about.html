The simple, unfair reality that led to GuideBuoy.

GuideBuoy AI exists because of a simple, unfair reality: after a scam, the person who has just lost money is the person expected to do the hardest work — retelling a painful story, in formal language, to a bank, then the Police, then maybe FIDReC, each with their own form, their own deadlines, their own jargon.

We think that burden should sit on the system, not on the victim. So we built Singapore's complaint helper: tell your story once, and let Lumi — our lighthouse AI — organise it into one report that every agency can use. A buoy doesn't pull you out of the water. It holds its position, keeps its light on, and shows you the way in. That's the product.
